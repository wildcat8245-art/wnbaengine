How to read this board: "Projection" is the fitted model's own point estimate (Poisson mean for assists, Negative Binomial mean for rebounds, quantile median for points/PRA) — not the number that decides the pick. The pick is decided by the full probability of clearing the sportsbook line, computed by whichever engine backtests better for that market: an empirical bootstrap Monte Carlo (resamples the player's own last 20 real games) for points, or the fitted parametric model for rebounds/assists/PRA. A skewed projection can legitimately sit on the opposite side of the line from the actual pick — that's flagged inline, not an error.

Stay-away flags override EV entirely: a confirmed injury "Out", a usage-vacuum conflict, or a trend-conflict (the player's real last-5 rate and minutes both moving opposite the model's side by ≥25%/≥15%) forces stake to $0 regardless of edge.
